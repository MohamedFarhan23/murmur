Murmur Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

Murmur turns your speech into text entirely on your phone. It doesn't collect, send or share any of your data. It doesn't even have permission to use the internet.

Microphone

Murmur asks for microphone access so it can transcribe what you say. It listens only while you're recording: after you tap the microphone button, or the Murmur home-screen widget. The audio is transcribed on your phone by a speech model built into the app, and is never sent anywhere.

What stays on your phone

  • Your current transcript. Its text is kept as a draft so it isn't lost if the app closes. Its audio is held in memory only, and is discarded unless you save a note.
  • Notes you save: their text and, if you choose, their audio.
  • Custom words you add, and your text-size setting.

All of this is stored in Murmur's private storage on your phone, where other apps can't read it. You can delete notes in the app at any time, and uninstalling Murmur deletes everything it stored.

What Murmur doesn't do

  • It has no internet access, so it can't upload anything.
  • It has no accounts, ads, analytics or crash reporting.
  • It doesn't share your data with anyone, including us.

Things you choose to do

When you copy text, it goes to your phone's clipboard. When you share text, it goes to the app you pick, and that app's privacy policy applies from then on.

Android backup

If you've turned on backup in your phone's settings, Android may include Murmur's stored data in your device backup to your Google account, as it does for most apps. That backup is managed by Android and Google, not by Murmur, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Children

Murmur isn't directed at children, and it doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone, since it collects none at all.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
