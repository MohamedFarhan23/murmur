Features

Small app. Surprisingly capable.

Everything you need to turn speech into text you can use, without handing your voice to anyone.

Tap any word to hear it or fix it

Words the model wasn't sure about are underlined. Tap one to play just that moment of audio, and pick the right word from sound-alike suggestions.

Privacy

Private by design, not by promise.

Most voice apps send your audio to a server. Murmur can't: Android never gave it permission to use the internet. Your words are processed on your phone and stay there.

Read the privacy policy →
  • No internet permissionThe only permission Murmur asks for is the microphone.
  • No accounts, ads or trackingNo analytics and no crash reporting. Nothing to sign up for.
  • Audio stays in memoryRecordings are only kept if you save a note and choose to keep its audio.
  • Open sourceDon't take our word for it. Read every line on GitHub.
How it works

From your voice to text, all on the phone.

A small pipeline keeps the model fed with speech, and only speech.

Listen

The microphone streams 16 kHz audio, held in memory only.

Filter

A voice detector and a pitch check pass on real speech, so silence and rustling don't turn into words.

Transcribe

Whistle, a 16.9 MB open speech model, runs on the Needle engine right on your phone.

Refine

Your custom words guide the model, and unsure words are marked for you to check.

Honestly

Is it perfect? No.

Running entirely on a phone is a trade-off between staying light and getting every word right. Here's where Murmur stands today.

English only

Other languages may come later.

Android only

For 64-bit Android phones. Built with Flutter, so other platforms are possible.

It mishears sometimes

Quiet speech and unusual names are hardest. That's what tap-to-fix and custom words are for.

Speak freely.

Free, open source and fully offline. Download the APK from GitHub today. Google Play is coming soon.
